Where education meets employment

Subir Baruah is an education, career development and youth employability professional based in Shillong. His work sits at the intersection of career counselling, skill development, job readiness, recruitment, training and workforce development.

He is the Founder & CEO of The Beginners, a Meghalaya-based organisation that works with students, graduates, educational institutions, employers and government-linked programmes to close the gap between education and employment.

Over the years, the work has grown from individual career counselling into the design and delivery of institutional, government, CSR and industry-linked programmes covering career awareness, employability training, digital skills, recruitment, assessments and placement support.
